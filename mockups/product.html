Kumkumadi Tailam is one of the oldest documented facial oils in Ayurvedic literature, first recorded in the Ashtanga Hridayam. Our version is hand-infused over 21 days using cold-pressed sesame oil as the base, with Kashmiri saffron added last to preserve its potency.

Unlike mass-market "brightening" oils, we don't rely on a single hero ingredient — the formula layers 24 herbs, each targeting a different stage of the pigmentation-repair process, from surface exfoliation to deep cellular turnover.

Full ingredient list, listed by concentration:

  • Sesame Oil (base)32%
  • Kashmiri Saffron8%
  • Sandalwood7%
  • Manjistha6%
  • Liquorice Root5%
  • Lotus Root5%
  • Vetiver4%
  • + 17 supporting herbs33%

Apply 3–4 drops to clean, slightly damp skin each night. Warm between palms first, then press gently into the face and neck — avoid rubbing, which can irritate Pitta-prone skin. Leave on overnight; follow with sunscreen if used in the morning.
