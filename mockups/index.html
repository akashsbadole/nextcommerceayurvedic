Dinacharya · Daily Ritual

Three steps, every sunrise

A minimal routine drawn from the Ashtanga Hridayam — order matters here, so we keep it exactly this simple.

01

Cleanse with Ubtan

A dry powder wash of chickpea flour, turmeric and rose — never a foaming cleanser that strips the skin barrier.

02

Nourish with Oil

Warm a few drops of Kumkumadi between your palms and press, don't rub, into skin still slightly damp.

03

Seal with Churna

A pinch of Ashwagandha in warm water or milk, taken before the day starts, to steady the nervous system.

Ingredient Spotlight

Ashwagandha, the vitality root

Withania somnifera has anchored Ayurvedic wellness formulas for over three thousand years, prized for helping the body adapt to daily stress. We source ours from a single cooperative of farmers in Nagpur who've grown it for three generations.
