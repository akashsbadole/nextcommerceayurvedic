Where It Began

A recipe book from Palakkad

In 1962, our founder's grandmother began recording the oil and churna recipes she'd learned from her own mother — measured in mutti (handfuls) and dina (days of infusion), never grams or hours. That book sat in a kitchen drawer for sixty years.

In 2019, three of her grandchildren — a chemist, a farmer, and a product designer — decided to translate it: keep the ratios and the process, add the lab testing modern regulation expects. Vedika is that translation.

What We Won't Compromise On

Four commitments

Formula Fidelity

We don't substitute ingredients for cheaper ones. If a herb isn't in season, the batch waits.

Traceable Sourcing

Every key ingredient is traced to a named farm or cooperative — we publish the list annually.

Third-Party Testing

Every batch is tested by an independent lab for heavy metals and microbial safety before it ships.

Honest Labelling

If a product isn't right for your dosha, our packaging says so instead of trying to sell you anyway.

Milestones

From kitchen drawer to lab bench

A real sequence of how the recipe book became a company — every step mattered to get the formulas right before scaling.

1962

The recipes are first recorded

Grandmother Lakshmi begins writing down oil and churna formulas passed down orally for two generations.

2019

Vedika is founded

Three grandchildren begin translating handful-and-day measurements into grams and hours, with a chemist verifying ratios.

2021

First independent lab partnership

Every formula is tested for heavy metals and microbial load before the first public batch ships.

2023

Farmer cooperative network established

Direct sourcing agreements signed with growers in Kerala, Nagpur and Kashmir for key botanicals.

2026

120+ formulations, PAN-India shipping

The current catalogue, still checked line by line against the original 1962 book.
